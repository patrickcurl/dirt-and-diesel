Test Dirt & Diesel

A start-from-nothing farming sim for Android. Help us test it before it launches — it takes about two minutes.

  1. Join the tester group

    Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone. Tap Join group — no approval needed, and we never post to it.

    Join the group

  2. Opt in on Google Play

    Open this link and tap Become a tester. It works on your phone or a computer.

    Become a tester

  3. Install it

    Get it from the Play Store like any other app. Updates arrive automatically.

    Open in Play Store

If step 2 says the app isn't available, check you're signed in with the account you joined the group with. If it still says that, testing hasn't opened yet — try again in a day. Works on Android phones, tablets and Chromebooks.
