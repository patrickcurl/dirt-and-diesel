Privacy Policy — Dirt & Diesel

Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version: Dirt & Diesel does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, no tracking, and makes no network connections of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not gather personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or usage analytics.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Dirt & Diesel has no networking code. It does not contact any server, including ours. It works identically with the device in aeroplane mode.

What is stored, and where

Your saved games, settings, and progress are written to your device's private app storage and stay there. They are not uploaded anywhere and are not readable by other apps. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no social logins, and no crash-reporting services.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. There is nothing to delete and no request to make.

Permissions

Dirt & Diesel requests no sensitive Android permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage outside its own private directory.

Changes

If a future version ever adds anything that collects or transmits data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described plainly here.

Contact

Questions about this policy: patrickwcurl@gmail.com

Dirt & Diesel is a single-player farming game. This page exists because app stores require a privacy policy URL, even for apps that collect nothing at all.
