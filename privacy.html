Privacy Policy — Dirt & Diesel

Last updated: 20 September 2026

The short version: we do not collect any personal information about you. The game keeps your farm on your own device. The one exception is optional rewarded ads: if you choose to watch one, Google's advertising service is involved, and it collects data as described below. Watching ads is entirely optional and the game is complete without them.

What we collect

Nothing. Dirt & Diesel has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no crash reporting. We do not gather your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any usage statistics, and we operate no server that could receive them.

Your saved games

Your farms, settings, trophy cabinet and family history are written to your device's private app storage and stay there. They are not uploaded anywhere and are not readable by other apps. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Optional rewarded ads

The game offers an optional reward: you may choose to watch a short video advert in exchange for a boost. You never have to. Everything an advert can give you can also be earned by playing, at the same rate and under the same limits, and the game is designed to be played without watching a single one.

These adverts are served by Google AdMob. When an advert is shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (a resettable ID, not your name)
  • IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model and operating system version
  • interaction with the advert, such as whether it was watched or closed

Google uses this to deliver and measure advertising, and to detect fraud. We do not receive any of it: we are told only that an advert was watched, so the game can award the boost. Google's own policy explains their handling in full:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Your choices about ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, which also lets you opt out of personalised advertising. If you are in a region that requires it, such as the European Economic Area or the UK, the game asks for your consent before any personalised advert is shown, and you may decline. Declining does not restrict the game in any way — you simply are not offered the optional reward, or are shown a non-personalised advert instead.

Children

Dirt & Diesel is not directed to children and is not listed in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us and we will look into it — though as described above, we hold no personal information at all.

Permissions

The game requests no sensitive Android permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or storage outside its own private directory. Network access is used only to fetch adverts you have chosen to watch.

Purchases

There are none. The game sells nothing, has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Changes

If a future version collects or transmits anything not described here, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described plainly.
